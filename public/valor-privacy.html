valor: privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

valor is a personal assistant built and used by one person, Ashutosh Sao, for his own job search. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, valor reads his own Gmail inbox using Google's read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It looks only for emails about his job applications (for example, confirmations, interview invitations and rejections). It cannot send, delete or change any email.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email headers are checked on the owner's own computer to find job-related messages; only those messages are read.
  • A short summary of each job-related email is stored on the owner's computer to track his applications and notify him.
  • To classify a job-related email, its text may be sent to a language-model provider (DeepSeek) under that provider's terms. Personal emails are not sent.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the owner's job search.

Google API data

valor's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
